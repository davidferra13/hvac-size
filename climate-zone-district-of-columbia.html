What the zone actually changes

Zone 4 works out to 21 BTU per square foot for cooling and 40 for heating. A contractor who sizes your system off square footage without asking where you live is using a number from somewhere else.

Sizing estimates using standard climate-zone load factors. A Manual J calculation is the definitive number and any contractor quoting a replacement should run one. Equipment sizing affects safety and efficiency; check local code.
