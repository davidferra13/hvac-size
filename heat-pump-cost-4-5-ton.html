Backup heat is a separate line

In a cold climate a heat pump needs supplemental heat, and that is not in the range above. Electric strip heat is the cheap install and the expensive winter. Pairing the heat pump with a gas furnace — dual fuel — costs more up front and less every January. See what your climate zone actually needs →

Rebates move this more than brand does

Federal and utility incentives on qualifying heat pumps are large enough to change which system is cheapest overall, and they change year to year. Check current programs before you sign, not after.

What to do with this number

Get three quotes. Use this range to spot the outlier, not to argue a contractor down to the bottom of it — the cheapest bid on a system that runs for fifteen years is rarely the one you want. A quote above this range is not automatically wrong; ask what is in it. Ductwork, electrical, a high-SEER2 unit, or a difficult attic all justify real money. A quote that cannot be explained is the one to walk away from.

Ask every bidder for the Manual J load calculation. A contractor sizing off square footage alone is guessing, and you are the one who lives with the guess.
